About me

Hi, I'm Sina, a 26-year-old from Iran.

My dream is to use my creativity and persistence to make the best videos I possibly can, with edits so good that anyone who watches steps out of a cold, tiring world for a moment and walks into what's going on in my head.

I was born in the year of the Rabbit, in the month of Azar, and I'm an INTP. That mix made me someone who is always chasing something that could change the world. What I love is those few minutes, or even seconds, in a video where you feel happy, get a rush, or even get emotional. For that moment you're inside something that makes you feel good.

Life in Iran is really hard right now, with soaring inflation and constant pressure. I support my family and I pay installments, so nothing is left to save. I can't afford a good PC and the other equipment I need to be my best.

I'd love your help so I can make great content for every taste, and I'll do everything I can so everyone enjoys it. Later I want to move into anime and films too. I'm also deeply into writing, and my mind is just twisted enough that I promise I'll make something that blows your mind.

I'll stay grateful, I'll show up every day, and for the rest of my life I'll keep making content you can enjoy for free. I love seeing a smile on your face, or a tear of emotion that calms your heart. I want everyone to feel understood, and to feel that this moment is the best one for them.

With love, Sina
